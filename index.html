Most of what I can do, I learned because a short needed it.

I picked up After Effects on my own because a project needed someone who knew it, and ended up leading its post-production. I've composed music for a short when nobody else could, and done clean-up on other people's animation, which taught me more about timing than animating my own scenes.

I've also learned that a note on a shot isn't a note on the person, from giving notes to classmates and from taking them.

I'm looking for an internship where I can keep learning from people who already do this every day.
